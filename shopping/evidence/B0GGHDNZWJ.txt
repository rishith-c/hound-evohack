About this item
[Comfortable Grip] Stop hurting your hands with bare metal! This fish weight scale features a specialized plastic handle, ensuring a comfort-grip experience even when hoisting a 110lb/50kg trophy catch. Rollin K
5 out of 5 stars
For the price, this scale couldn't be beat.
Reviewed in the United States on July 25, 2026
Verified Purchase

For the price, I was quite surprised by this scale having a backlit display with large, readable numbers. I once repaired 100's of digital postal scales, so I know what is required in a scale.

I purchased this scale so I could test the amount of force required to open an exterior door that has a hydraulic door closer. I felt the door required too much effort to open, so I purchased this scale for testing the amount of pull required. Since I live in a retirement community, I must know, in pounds, how much force is required to open an exterior solid-core door. In a community such as this, the published force required is around 5 pounds. I made a simple string sling that I could loop around the exterior door pull-handle. The force required is measured after the door has been opened by a few degrees. After I looped the rope around the exterior handle, I opened the door slightly before I read the pull force. Since I could not measure the push force required from the inside, I felt measuring the pull force would be the same. Once inertia was overcome, the scale indeed measured about 5 pounds of pull force required. It always seemed like too much force to push it open, while my method of measurement did not exactly follow how building code required the force to be measured; it still gave me the information I was looking for.
