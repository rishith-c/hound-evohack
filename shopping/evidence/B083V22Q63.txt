Product description

Product Description:

Product Name: USB-A to USB-C Charging Cable (USB A to Type C Cable)

Brand: etguuds

Color: Gray

Material: Nylon braided

Feature: USB-C Cable supports charging speeds of up to 18W (9V/2A,12V/1.5A), and data transfer speeds of 480Mbps (USB 2.0). The USBC cord uses a User-Friendly Connector, reversible connector allows plugging into all USB Type-C compatible devices

Choose this color: The color of this USB A to USB C cable is classic gray nylon braided. Gray is also resistant to dirt, which can reduce fingerprints and stains left in daily use, keeping the cable clean and beautiful

Fast Charging Warm Tips:

1.This is a USB-A to USB-C cable, NOT USB-C to USB-C cable.

2.If your device's original cable is a USB-C to USB-C cable, then only a USB-C to USB-C cable can charge your device as fast as possible.

3.This cord WILL NOT provide you with fast charging alone, you will need a wall charger for fast charging and a phone capable of the same.

4.Friendly Tips: You will get etguuds USB A to USB C nylon braided cable, please confirm the length of the cables before buying, the length of the included cable connector.
