【Professional Function Design】This 60w 110v pencil-type soldering iron features adjustable temperatures(392 ℉-842 ℉/200-450 ℃) and thermostatic setting. In addition, our soldering iron with ON/OFF Switch, which is easy to save energy and ensure safe welding.

I bought this months ago and never wrote a review. After a router incident I decided to. It is a good first soldering kit. It comes with the basic materials and more space to add more later. The heat is low grade wire and jewelry levels. Good for basic electronics but anything above replacing a battery in a cartridge or low level electrical wiring but nothing advanced. The safety is included so when the iron is hot you can safely place it on a plate or ceramic tile with the enclosed holder. For the price you get a good quality starter kit nothing like my old industrial one but it has done the jobs I bought it for. If you’re looking for something that is low cost and good for older consoles modding and repair this is it.

5 out of 5 stars
Good for quick fixes
Reviewed in the United States on November 11, 2025
Color: 60w 10-in-1 New
Verified Purchase

I am a complete beginner to soldering, and needed to solder some pins on an SBC game console. This soldering iron worked well for my needs. I appreciate that a knob controls and shows the temperature rather than a digital display with buttons. I also like how it is wired, as I will seldom use it and do not see the need for a wireless soldering iron. There's quite a few tips included, and it is easy to swap tips. The iron heats up pretty quickly as well.

Now, I cannot say if this soldering iron is any good for more involved projects, as I just needed it for a quick fix. But if you're needing a soldering iron for just occasional, quick usage, I think this one is pretty good.

The included flux paste was completely dried out, but I'm not even taking off a star because this soldering iron is already so cheap so I consider that an extra more than anything. I was just mildly inconvenienced because I needed to order more flux and wait on it. So I would say that if you purchase this soldering iron, be sure to purchase more flux along with it.
