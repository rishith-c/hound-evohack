Product Description

Introducing the SKYRC B6AC Neo Smart Charger: Revolutionize your battery charging experience with the SKYRC B6AC Neo, a high-performance charger designed for a wide range of batteries. Whether you're an RC enthusiast, drone pilot, or electronic hobbyist, this charger caters to LiPo, LiFe, LiIon, LiHV, NiMH, and NiCd batteries, showcasing its unmatched versatility.

Efficient and Speedy Charging: The B6AC Neo excels in its charging capabilities, offering 200W of power when connected to DC and 60W on AC. This power distinction ensures a rapid charging process, significantly reducing downtime and enhancing your productivity. Charge a LiPo 6S 1300mAh battery in just 20 minutes on AC or a mere 10 minutes on DC, showcasing its impressive speed.

Advanced Discharging and Battery Checking: Beyond charging, the B6AC Neo serves as an efficient discharger and battery checker. When connected to its balance port, it offers detailed insights into cell voltage and balance state across various battery types. It's an essential tool for maintaining battery health, longevity, and safety, especially for LiPo batteries known for their high energy density and lightweight properties.

Digital Power Supply Capabilities: Stepping beyond conventional chargers, the B6AC Neo doubles as a digital power supply. With an adjustable voltage range of 2-27V and a current range of 1-10A, it caters to various applications, ensuring optimal power delivery. Safety is a top priority, with protections against short circuit, overload, and reverse polarity.

Versatile and Reliable: The charger automatically switches between Constant Voltage (CV) and Constant Current (CC) modes, adapting to different charging needs. This versatility is crucial for efficiently powering a range of devices and for effectively charging lithium-ion batteries.

Unmatched Speed and Compatibility: Its capability extends to powering diverse devices like the Nintendo Switch and the latest MacBook Pro, delivering unparalleled charging speed and efficiency. Expect 3x faster charging for iPhones and full-speed charging for the latest Samsung phones. It's not just a charger, but a complete power solution for your modern gadgets.

Enhanced Protection and User-Friendly Interface: The B6AC Neo is equipped with multiple safety features, including over-voltage, over-temperature, and capacity protection. The ColorX interface, with its responsive menu and well-designed icons, makes operation intuitive and accessible in multiple languages. The quiet operation of the charger's cooling fan ensures a disturbance-free environment, making it ideal for use in any setting.

Continuous Improvement: Stay up-to-date with the latest features and enhancements through effortless firmware upgrades via the Charger Master App. These upgrades ensure improved stability, performance, and compatibility, keeping your charger at the forefront of technology.

Cool little cube that is both a charger/discharger and a power supply
Reviewed in the United States on August 22, 2024
Verified Purchase

I don't usually write reviews until some time has passed, but this little charger is... well, cool. First it functions as a very small single battery charger. Next - if you have other devices that need DC power - it can act as a DC power supply! That's a unique capability, and I actually find that I used it to power my similarly diminutive icharger (single), which does not have the ability to be powered by AC current. The B6AC turns into a DC power supply and allowing the extra features of the icharger to be engaged. It also allows me to power many other devices (fans, lights, etc). The display is very clear, and the buttons are a nice change from the rotary "barrel-button" on some other charges, which are unpredictable. This things buttons move precisely one setting per press, but you can move rapidly if you hold the button down for a few seconds. It isn't able to deliver much amperage if you are trying to charge 6 and 8 cell batteries, but it gets the job done. A good purchase.
