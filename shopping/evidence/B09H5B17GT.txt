About this item
✿M3 Roll in T Nut: This M3 spring t nut can be used to connect 20mm aluminum extrusion parts, suitable for european standard 2020 series aluminum extrusion profile rail. The drop in t nut’s spring ball loaded design can fix the t slot nut in a suitable position.
✿High-Quality Material: M3 t slot nut is made of high-quality 8.8 grade carbon steel, strong and durable. The surface is nickel-plated, rust-proof and corrosion-resistant.
✿Wide Range of Application: M3 2020 t slot nut is suitable for aluminum extrusion profile with slot 6mm, T/V slot 20mm series aluminum rail accessories, and is widely used in the construction of 3D printers, CNC routers, robotics projects and industrial aluminum frame structures.

The M5 spring T-nut works as advertised. Its nice spare items to have when building/modding a 3D printer where the slide T-nut was not installed into the frame. Most of the spring T-nut went in without a hitch. However, few pieces need little more "force" to slot into the 2020 extrusion. If it doesn't slide in with your fingers, the easiest way to insert these T-nut is to put some pressure on the ball of the T-nut, screw in the M5 screw (30-40mm long ones) and pull/push on the M5 screw to insert it into the 2020 extrusion.
