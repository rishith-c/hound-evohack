About this item
1243-Piece Dupont Connector & Crimping Tool Kit: Includes a 2030M ratcheting Dupont crimper, an instruction manual, and 1243 Dupont 2.54mm connectors, all neatly stored in a high-quality double-layer storage box. Configured with a specific 500 female to 150 male crimp pin ratio to match actual consumption rates in custom wire harnesses, minimizing leftover plastic housings
Crimping Range & Compatibility: The 2030M ratcheting crimper features a 32-20 AWG (0.03-0.5 mm²) capacity. Specifically designed for micro-connectors, it is suitable for JST series (JWPF 2.0 mm, PH 2.0 mm, XH/SYP/SM 2.5 mm), Dupont 2.54 mm, KF2510, Molex series (Micro-Fit 3.0 mm, KK 254), and TE series terminals
24-Type Dupont Connector Kit: Includes single-row (1P to 10P) and dual-row (2x2P to 2x6P) black nylon housings, jumper caps, and male/female terminals. Equipped with female socket headers and breakaway pin headers (single- and dual-row, straight and right-angle) to provide the required mating interfaces for both Wire-to-Board and Wire-to-Wire connections
Nylon Housings & Tin-Plated Terminals: The connector housings are molded from Nylon for impact resistance and reliable latching. The male and female crimp terminals are manufactured from tin-plated copper, providing oxidation resistance and maintaining low-resistance electrical contact for your custom cables (ribbon cable/wire not included)
Electronic Wiring Applications: Standard 2.54mm pitch components used for prototyping with Arduino and Raspberry Pi, wiring 3D printer mainboards, custom PC sleeving, and building arcade gaming machines. Suitable for creating sensor cables, power leads, and logic board interfaces
