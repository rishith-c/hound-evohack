About this item
【Zeee 3S 5200mAh Lipo Specification】- Battery Voltage: 11.1V; Configuration:3S1P; Capacity: 5200mAh; Discharge: 50C; Connector: XT60 Plug. Note: This battery features an XT60 connector. Please confirm your device's compatibility before purchase.
【5200mAh Battery Dimensions】- Zeee 11.1V 50C 5200mAh rc lipo battery dimension is: 132*43*25mm/5.2*1.69*0.98 inch(L*W*H); Approx Weight(±15g): 343g /12.1oz.
【3S RC Lipo Application】- Zeee 3S 5200mah lipo battery compatible with RC helicopter, airplane, multirotor, Boat, Car, Truck, RC models 1/10 scale vehicles, Slash VXL, Slash 4x4 VXL, E-Maxx Brushless, racing models(Only if the voltage, dimension and the plug match, then it will fit)
【Safety Warning】- 1. Users must read safety manual before use of the battery; 2. You must use Lithium Polymer(LiPo) specific balance charger only; 3. The suggested charge rate is 0.5 - 1C; 4. Never charge the battery unattended; 5. The ambient temperature for lithium battery charging should be maintained between 41°F and 95°F.
【How to Choose a Charger】- 1. The charger must have a balance charging function; 2. The charger must support 3S battery charging(11.1V nominal, 12.6V fully charged); 3. The recommended charging current for this battery is 2.6A to 5.2A, so the charger must support this range.
