Product Description

SKYRC B6AC V2 is the upgraded version for well-known iMAX B6AC. Compared with iMAX B6AC, it is more accurate and stable and also has some new features and functions. Users could set the terminal voltage by themselves and connect it to PC for PC control and firmware upgrade. What's more, users could also use it as Lithium Battery Meter and Battery Internal Resistance Meter. There are Automatic Charging Current Limit, Capacity Limit, Temperature Threshold and Processing Time Limit which makes the charger safer than V1. SKYRC B6AC V2 is a high-performance, micro processor control charge/discharge station with battery management suitable for use with all current battery types, with integral equalizer for six-cell Lithium- Polymer (LiPo), Lithium High Voltage,Lithium iron phosphate(LiFe) and Lithium-Ion (LiIon) batteries; maximum 6A charge current and maximum 50W charge power.

This Charger is more than a charger, it is a battery saver. Best money I have spent and would recommend this to anyone who needs a balance charger. Not only can this charger charge your multi cell batteries it can also re-balance those cells too.

I had a 3 cell battery that was unbalanced 1 full cell and 2 storage level cells because of a defective smart charger. I was wondering how I was going to bring this battery back to a balanced charge. I asked around and got many different ideas but decided to buy this charger anyway. Turns out this charger will bring your battery back into a balanced charge. It will take some time depending on how out of balance they are but it does work. I watched this charger and what it does to bring the balance back is to trickle charge the battery.

One thing worth noting is if you are buying this to charge your airsoft batteries you are going to need an airsoft adapter which is not included. Double check that the adapter you are buying is for airsoft as the ones for RC do not work on the airsoft batteries.

Nice charger and feels like quality
Reviewed in the United States on August 23, 2017
Verified Purchase

This charger has every appearance of being an original and the SN checks out on the IMAX web site. It does everything they say it will and it was not too difficult to figure everything out although the instructions could be a bit clearer. They make perfect sense once you know what your doing but in the beginning you may be scratching your head a bit. The only reason I gave it 4 stars instead of 5 is because they have put a filter capacitor across the power input which in itself is a good thing but when you first plug the unit in you get an arc at the outlet because the capacitor is drawing so much power for a millisec to charge up. They really need a soft on circuit or at least an on/off switch. It probably will not do any damage but is disconcerting to get that snap and flash every time you plug in.
