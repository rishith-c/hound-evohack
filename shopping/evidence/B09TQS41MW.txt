Underrated, cheap capacitors
Reviewed in the United States on April 29, 2026
Verified Purchase

Don't buy these capacitors, remember what they say, you get what you pay for. Some of these capacitors are only 440UF. These are the 35 volt 470UF caps. A good capacitor will have a UF reading over what it's supposed to be not under. Make sure you test every capacity you buy no matter who you buy it from before installing it. One time I bought 2,000 capacitors and all of them was so bad they registered on the meter like a resistor, I'm lucky I didn't install them in my project.
