Picked this up for a Raspberry Pi 4, and it’s been a solid performer.

Boot times are reasonable, and overall responsiveness is good for general use. It handles the OS, updates, and typical workloads without any issues.

Transfer speeds are what you’d expect—fast enough for everyday tasks, even if it’s not a high-end performance card.

Things to know:

Works well for Pi OS and general projects

Stable—no random disconnects or corruption issues so far

Not designed for heavy write-intensive workloads (like constant logging or database use long-term)

If you’re doing something write-heavy, you might want a high-endurance or performance-tier card. But for typical Raspberry Pi use, this gets the job done.

Bottom line:

Reliable, no-nonsense microSD for a Raspberry Pi 4. Good balance of cost and performance for everyday use.
