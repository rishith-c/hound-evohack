About this item
Complete Accessory Kit: Get everything you need for your project in one package, including 4 flange shaft couplings, a mini hex wrench, and 8 fastening screws.
Material: Flange coupling crafted from high-quality 45# steel, these couplings are heat-treated and electroplated for exceptional strength and corrosion resistance, ensuring long-lasting performance.
Precision Fit: Designed with high-precision technology, our flange shaft coupler ensure a secure and stable connection between shafts, preventing separation during operation for reliable use.
Wide application: Motor shaft coupler can fix the shaft on the plane, and can easily install other accessories on the shaft, widely used in model shaft connection, perfect accessories for RC model motors, etc.
Effortless Installation: Each flange shaft coupling pairs perfectly with an included mini hex wrench, making installation and replacement straightforward and hassle-free for any model enthusiast.
