Patrick Erley
3 out of 5 stars
Default setup not ideal
Reviewed in the United States on May 29, 2024

I purchased a bunch of these(40) to do some testing with matter and BLE mesh. It was not obvious out of the box how to use the ws2812 led. There's actually a solder pad you must jumper over in order to activate the LED. After that, it's just a standard ws2812 on GPIO 48. They also used a somewhat odd USB serial bridge (QinHeng Electronics USB Single Serial) but it's a standard cdc_acm device, so just needed to sort out user permissions for that.

If the board had come with the rgb jumper soldered I would have given it 4 stars, or if the jumper were labeled 'rgb enable' instead of rgb, I would have also given it 4. To get 5 stars, they would need to do either of those, as well as labeling what pin the RGB is connected to.
