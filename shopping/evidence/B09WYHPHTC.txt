About this item
HIGH-QUALITY MATERIAL - This 12-inch hack saw is made of high-strength aluminum alloy Material, thick, high hardness and heavy-duty durability. In addition, our hacksaw blades are made of Bi-metal material, having high strength and good toughness.
ADJUSTABLE AND REPLACEABLE DESIGN - The hacksaw blades can be set as 45 degrees or 90 degrees according to different situations and meet your different needs. The knob also can adjust the tightness of the hacksaw frame for saving time and labor.
COMFORTABLE RUBBER HANDLE - This hacksaw has a TPR material handle, compared with other ordinary rubber materials, TPR has better wear resistance and skid resistance, reduces hand pressure when cutting and the size will work with any hand size.
MULTI-FUNCTION HANDSAW - The hacksaw for cutting metal also tackles a variety of other daily basic cutting tasks, suitable for cutting wood, PVC, laminates, tree branches and other materials. Widely used in gardening, decoration, wood processing, DIY maintenance, etc.
WHAT YOU WILL GET - This package includes a premium aluminum alloy hacksaw frame, 2PC 24T Bi-Metal blade, suitable for sawing metal, plastic; 1PC 18T Bi-Metal blade, suitable for sawing wood.
