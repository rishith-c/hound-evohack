NickL
4 out of 5 stars
Similar to an EX330 in accuracy for a lot less coin
Reviewed in the United States on July 27, 2026
Style: 600V Large Screen
Verified Purchase

tl;dr: There's nothing particularly special about this multimeter, but it's a good value if you need something for basic measurements and want true RMS.

My existing EX330 has been occasionally acting up, so I decided to get a meter so I could semi-retire it. After a bit of searching, I decided to give this Westinghouse multimeter a try. In terms of accuracy specs it's very similar to the EX330 with one notable exception: It has significantly lower resolution and accuracy when it comes to frequency measurement than the EX330, which is why I'm only semi-retiring the EX330. The build quality feels decent and the display is easy to read.

It does have a two advantages that stand out over the EX330. For one, this multimeter has true RMS, while the EX330 lacks it. Another advantage is that it has a latching buzzer, while the EX330 doesn't. The included probes are not terrible and are reasonably sharp, but I would recommend getting a nice set of gold plated silicone probes such as ones from ProbeMaster. It really makes a difference when it comes to things such as when you're on the continuity mode (much more consistent results than the included probes based on my testing.)

I do wish 2 AAAs were used as opposed to a 9v (minor gripe.) The included 9v appears to be a carbon-zinc battery. While that may be a cost cutting measure, carbon-zinc batteries are much less likely to leak vs. alkaline, which means it's less likely to ruin the meter. Some others have complained about how hard it was to install the battery, but I only found it to be slightly finicky.

While this multimeter doesn't have any features that stands out such as having super high accuracy or have a high count, it's an excellent value if you need a multimeter for basic measurements and want true RMS.

SWI
3 out of 5 stars
not the quality that you would expect from a major brand
Reviewed in the United States on March 31, 2025
Style: 600V Large Screen
Vine Customer Review of Free Product

Westinghouse is up there with just a few other centuries-old companies that everyone has grown to respect. This product is charging more and delivering less than you would expect.

I was pleased to see that it came with leads. The listing and photos didn't mention that.

Installing the battery requires a tiny screwdriver. Then you have to pry open a plastic cover that feels really wrong, like it's going to break or that you're doing something wrong.

One very common feature that I use frequently and have come to expect from a volt meter is the ability to ring out wires to find a short. This can be achieved with the Ohms setting, but I like a meter that has "Continuity" mode, with a ringer, an audible tone telling you there is electrical continuity. This device claims continuity mode, but when you touch the probes together, it does not ring.

I have not tested other features yet but from out of the box, this will go to the back of the toolbox and only come out if other (less expensive) meters are dead.

I removed a star for the battery case and removed another star for the lack of functioning Continuity mode (and yes, I did press the "MODE" button to switch Continuity on, though the instructions were not clear about that).
