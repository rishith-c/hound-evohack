=== DIAGRAM 1-power: Hound 1 — power: pack → shield → one buck (servo + fan, sheet 3); Pi on its bank ===

CONNECTORS (pin order is the physical order on the part):
  PACK — HOOVO 3S 2200 mAh 80C LiPo — T-plug (Deans)
     pins: V+ | GND
     NOTES: 11.1 V nominal, 12.6 V full, 10.5 V floor. No divider, no alarm: 30-minute timer, then meter it. A pack that reads 6–7 V is dead: never charge it.
  PLUG — Deans pull-plug on the deck (OliRC 14 AWG extension) = the E-STOP
     pins: pack + | pack − | rover + | rover −
     NOTES: The mating pair sits at the deck's rear corner where a hand can reach it. Pulling it apart kills everything on the pack side; the Pi keeps running on the bank.
  SHIELD_PWR — CNC Shield v3 — power screw terminal (the only pack splice)
     pins: VMOT | GND
     NOTES: Two wires per screw: the 14 AWG pack lead and the 22 AWG XL4015 input wire, twisted together before insertion, tug-tested. 1000 µF 25 V electrolytic across VMOT/GND at the terminal, stripe (−) to GND.
  XL4015 — HiLetgo XL4015 5 A buck — trim OUT to 5.0 V
     pins: IN+ | IN− | OUT+ | OUT−
     NOTES: HiLetgo board: four big solder pads, IN+/IN− at the coil end, OUT+/OUT− at the other — tin them and solder the 22 AWG wires (no screw terminals). Set 5.00 V on the meter with nothing on OUT (pot turns counter-clockwise to lower). 4 A continuous, 5 A peak. OUT goes to the PCA9685 terminal, which distributes it to both servos and the fan. 5.0 V (not 6) so the fan can share this rail; the MG996R runs 4.8–7.2 V.
  BANK — USB power bank 5 V / 3 A (logic island)
     pins: USB-A out
     NOTES: Genuine 3 A bank. Cable ≤ 0.2 m with 22 AWG or heavier power conductors (a short '3 A fast-charge' cable). Powers ONLY the Pi; the Pi powers the R4, the hub, the adapters, the radars and the webcam.
  PI_PWR — Raspberry Pi 4 — USB-C power in
     pins: USB-C
     NOTES: Under-voltage warning at 4.63 V. Early Pi 4 (rev 1.1) needs an A-to-C or non-e-marked cable.

     NOTES: Plugs onto channel 2. It cannot hold an angle: the firmware only nudges it, eased timed turns, then pulse cut; 'p' stops. This one turns right below 1500 µs (PAN_SIGN −1). Stop-pulse trim 'T' is set once with the remote's hold-still test.
  PCA_CH15 — PCA9685 channel 15 — used as a 5 V tap
     pins: PWM | V+ | GND
     NOTES: Only V+ and GND are used: the fan's red to V+, black to GND. PWM pin stays empty.

     pins: 5V (red) | GND (black)

=== PROCEDURES AND WARNINGS (print these on the sheet; they come from the electrical model wiring.py) ===
  [WARN] V_SERVO (XL4015 trimmed to 5.0 V → PCA9685 V+ terminal (tilt ch 3, pan ch 2, fan on ch 15's V+/GND pins)): avg 0.75 A of 4.0 A (19%), peak 4.40 A of 5.0 A (88%)

  [WARN] A 1 m 28 AWG phone cable to the Pi would drop 1.03 V → 3.87 V at the Pi = brownout. The bank cable is a spec: ≤ 0.2 m with 22 AWG or heavier power conductors (a short '3 A fast-charge' cable)
  [WARN] Motor phases run 1.5 A through Dupont 2.54 contacts rated 2.0 A — inside rating but the crimp quality is the margin. A contact that heats in the wheels-up test gets re-crimped or soldered.
  [WARN] Both servos stalling together = 4.4 A on the XL4015 (5 A peak, 4 A continuous) and 2.1 A per PCA9685 channel header (rated 2.0 A): momentary by nature; keep stalls short with the firmware limits (tilt 78–150°) and release the servos ('t -1', 'p -1') between stations
  [WARN] No EN pull-up fitted: during an R4 reset (~1 s at boot, every sketch upload) all four drivers are ENABLED at full holding current. Harmless for a second; NOT harmless if you flash repeatedly with the pack plugged in (drivers heat). So: pack unplugged whenever the USB cable is doing anything but driving.
  [WARN] No fuse, no cell alarm, no voltage divider (all declined for simplicity): nothing stops the pack from over-discharging. Rule: a full pack (12.6 V) gives ~45 min of driving — set a 30-minute timer, then meter the pack; stop at 11.1 V, never below 10.5 V. A pack that reads 6–7 V is dead (pack A).
  [WARN] Pi 5 only: default 600 mA USB cap → set usb_max_current_enable=1, or use the Pi 4 this file assumes.
  [WARN] bank → Pi USB-C cable (SPEC: ≤ 0.2 m, 22 AWG or heavier power conductors): 22 AWG × 0.2 m, peak 2.41 A (ampacity 7 A, USB-C 3.0 A), round-trip drop 51 mV = 1.0%
  [WARN] Pi USB-A → webcam (captive cable, 1.5 m): 28 AWG × 1.5 m, peak 0.30 A (ampacity 1.4 A, USB-A 1.5 A), round-trip drop 192 mV = 3.8%
  [VERIFY] Load-test the bank at 2.5 A for 60 s with the meter on the Pi's 5 V pin: below 4.8 V the bank is not a 3 A bank.
  [VERIFY] If the bank's output is USB-C and the Pi 4 is an early board (pre-2020, rev 1.1), an e-marked C-to-C cable will not power it — use an A-to-C cable or a plain C-to-C. Later Pi 4s and all Pi 5s are fine
  [VERIFY] Before connecting SDA/SCL: power each I2C module alone and meter SDA to GND — it must read ≤ 3.3 V. A module whose pull-ups go to 5 V goes on 3.3 V power or not on the Pi.
  [VERIFY] One I2C bus for everything: the Pi's 1.8 kΩ pull-ups plus the boards' own 10 kΩ. i2cdetect -y 1 must show 0x33 (thermal) and 0x29 (rear ToF); the front ToF appears at 0x30 only after sensing/tof.py re-addresses it (both XSHUT low, FRONT alone → 0x30, then REAR).   [VERIFY] The tilt servo must be a 180° (positional) MG996R: with the remote, set a tilt and let go — it must stop dead. A 360° servo keeps turning under a tilt command (it reads the pulse as speed), cannot hold the camera, and winds one way under the show. TowerPro sells both with the same case
  [PROCEDURE] 1000 µF electrolytic across VMOT: stripe = negative, to the GND terminal. Reversed, it vents on the first power-up
  [PROCEDURE] CSI v2: ESP32 #2 runs csi_tx OFF the rover (dropped as far along the wall as the room allows, any USB 5 V, channel 6, 100 Hz probes).   [PROCEDURE] DRV8825 into the shield socket with its EN pin over the socket's EN label (the trimpot ends up toward the shield's power terminal on genuine Pololu layouts; clones vary — go by the label). Backwards = dead driver on power-up, no warning
  [PROCEDURE] The 40 mm 5 V fan plugs onto the PCA9685's channel-15 header: red → the middle (V+) pin, black → the bottom (GND) pin. The XL4015 is set to 5.0 V for exactly this; the tilt servo is fine at 5 V.
  [PROCEDURE] Servo/fan return: XL4015 OUT−, which is the same node as IN− (a buck shares its negative) and so the shield GND screw — the D11 signal is referenced to it automatically. The LD2410Cs return through their adapters (USB side), the LD2450 through the Pi header's pin 34. Pack current never flows through a board GND pin or a USB cable.
  [PROCEDURE] The Pi header has two 3V3 pins and one SDA/SCL pair, but four boards want 3V3 and three want SDA/SCL — one Dupont female per pin. Chain through the boards' second header/STEMMA port (Adafruit-pattern MLX/ToF boards have one), or use a mini breadboard as the 3V3/SDA/SCL splitter. Never force two females onto one pin.
  [PROCEDURE] AS BUILT (v1.0 best drive): DRV8825 1/32 = M0 + M2 → caps on MS1 and MS3 under each driver, MS2 open (eight caps), plus the D12 and D13 jumpers on the A header = ten jumpers total. Caps go on before the drivers. 1/16 (one cap, MS3 only, firmware m 16) is the faster alternative — a drive-setting change, ask first.
  [PROCEDURE] The shield's VMOT/GND screw terminal is the only pack splice: the 14 AWG pack lead and the 22 AWG XL4015 input wire share each screw (2.4 mm² total, inside the 5.08 mm terminal's range). Strip 7 mm, twist the two conductors together before inserting, tug-test both.
  [PROCEDURE] Motor coil pairs by multimeter: the two leads that show ~2 Ω are one coil. A coil split across the 2B/2A–1A/1B header halves makes a motor buzz and not turn.
  [PROCEDURE] Each LD2410C is a 4-wire job to its own CP2102: VCC → the adapter's +5V pin (never 3V3 — too weak, and the radar needs 5 V), GND → GND, TX → RXD, RX → TXD.   [PROCEDURE] Power-up: bank → Pi → R4 (USB) FIRST, then the pack at the T-plug. Power-down and BEFORE every flash/reset of the R4: pull the pack plug first.   [PROCEDURE] PCA9685 on the CNC shield's comms header: shield SDA → PCA SDA, SCL → SCL, 5V → VCC, GND → GND (5 V I2C from the R4). XL4015 OUT+/OUT− → the PCA9685's V+/GND screw terminal. === DECISION ALREADY MADE: which bus drives the PCA9685 ===
The PCA9685 hangs on the CNC shield's SDA/SCL comms header (R4 A4/A5, 5 V I2C, VCC from the shield's 5V pin) and is driven by the R4 (firmware 't <deg>' for tilt; pan is a continuous-rotation servo driven only by 'n dir ms' eased timed nudges then pulse off, as in Ceres's turret_duet.ino; nothing is driven at boot). XL4015 OUT → its V+ terminal. Tilt servo on channel 3, pan on channel 2, fan on channel 15's V+/GND pins. It must never also touch the Pi's SDA/SCL. Draw sheet 3 exactly so.

=== SELF-CHECK (run it, print PASS/FAIL per line) ===
1. Every CONNECTOR:pin used in CONNECTIONS exists in that connector's pin list.
2. Each LD2410C has exactly ONE GND wire, to its own CP2102's GND, and its VCC from that adapter's +5V pin (never its 3V3 pin). The LD2450 has one GND wire, to Pi pin 34, and its 5V from Pi pin 4.
