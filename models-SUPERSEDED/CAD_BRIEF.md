# Mecanum hackathon demonstrator — CAD brief

Latest user scope: mostly intact floors beside rubble, for a hackathon. The user explicitly requested CAD after the preliminary screening report; these files are a provisional concept, not a passed build release.

Coordinates: millimetres; X forward, Y left, Z upward, floor Z=0. Four 350 mm 2020 rails; 250 mm wheelbase, 480 mm wheel-centre track, illustrative 100 mm mecanum wheels in opposite-handed diagonal pairs; four direct-drive JK42HS40-1704-13A envelopes; fifth motor on a transverse 200 mm sensor sweep. Battery low on the deck, Arduino/CNC Shield/A4988 with cooling, external TB6600, Raspberry Pi, two ESP32-S3 reservations, two forward-facing LD2410C reservations.

Pre-model screening (not verified operating values): reserve 6.0 kg; 100 mm wheels; 0.1 m/s speed; 0.1 m/s² acceleration; carpet Crr=0.08. Use an explicit 0.65 wheel/roller loss allowance for screening, not a measured efficiency. On level ground F=6×9.81×0.08+6×0.1=5.3088 N. Wheel torque F×0.05/(4×0.65)=0.10209 N·m; a 1.5 ratio requires measured dynamic motor torque ≥0.15314 N·m at 19.10 rpm. At 1/4 stepping, 254.65 pulses/s/wheel, 1018.59 pulses/s total. No measured torque has been supplied. The old fixed-wheel lateral-scrub equation is not a mecanum turning model and is not reused as such. Lateral motion, yaw resistance, traction and motor heating remain test gates.

Static bracket pure-moment screening only: supplied M=0.40 N·m=400 N·mm; trial rectangular width b=54 mm, thickness t=6 mm; section modulus bt²/6=324 mm³; nominal stress M/Z=1.235 MPa; against supplied 30 MPa working stress, ratio=24.3, MS=23.3. This is not a part-level structural pass: holes, gussets, stress concentrations, clamp force, shaft overhang, side loads, layer direction and hot-material properties are excluded. No positive generic beam result overrides those gaps.

Expected assembly envelope: approximately 350 mm fore/aft ×520 mm wide ×280 mm high. Check actual exported bounds. Minimum motor/body clearance around 28 mm; motor-bracket lower edge 23 mm. Deck tiles are 170 mm square; guide bridge is a purchased/sheet part or needs a later split-print design. CAD does not establish obstacle capability.

Catalogue provenance: step.parts exact JK42HS40-1704-13A, JK42HS40 and JKONG NEMA17 searches returned no match. CNC Shield, TB6600 and LD2410C also returned none. Generic mecanum wheel and MGN12H entries were downloaded with SHA-256 verification into references; their geometry is a simple envelope. Pi 4 catalogue STEP was also downloaded and checksum checked; its 52 MB detailed model is replaced in this assembly with a labelled envelope for interactive responsiveness. Pi model is not confirmed. ESP32-S3 WROOM modules exist in the catalogue but are not the unknown user's development boards. All these envelopes remain explicitly labelled; catalogue availability does not identify the hardware owned or the wheel hub interface.

Validation targets: closed valid solids, valid STEP roundtrip, four wheel centres and opposite diagonal roller handedness, motor 31 mm mounting pitch and 22.2 mm pilot clearance, positive motor/frame and wheel/mount clearances, a 200 mm sweep without head/mast collisions, exported separate custom-part STEP/STL, and saved assembly snapshots. These are CAD checks only. Full assembly fasteners, bearings, roller internals, wiring, flexible cable routing and final purchased part interfaces are incomplete.

The LD2410C is specified by its manufacturer as an indoor presence sensor with up-to-6 m detection and 0.75 m distance resolution. Its standard output and a short translation baseline do not establish accurate through-rubble localization. WiFi CSI also needs a known working RF arrangement and calibration; two boards plus CAD are not a demonstrated detector. Sources: https://www.hlktech.com/en/Goods-239.html and https://static.jkongmotor.com/JK42HS40-1704-13A-aidlnBprKrkloSRilikkrllkn.pdf .
